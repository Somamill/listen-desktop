Welcome to listen. This is an original demonstration recording, created for testing the audiobook player. It is not an extract from a commercial audiobook. This first chapter helps you try the normal listening controls. Press play to begin, pause when you need a break, and resume from the same position. The player remembers your place in each book. You can move backward or forward by the skip intervals you choose in Settings. You can also change playback speed. Add a bookmark to mark an important moment, then open the bookmarks list to return to it. The sample stays in your own library and can be played offline once it has been imported. The next chapter describes chapters, metadata, and conversion.
