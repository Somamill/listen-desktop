This is the second chapter of the listen demonstration. Audio files can be imported from Files, downloaded from a direct HTTPS address that you are authorized to use, or brought from your own media server. No listen account, payment, or subscription is required for local playback. You can edit a book's title and author, organize it into folders, and build a listening queue. Multiple MP3 tracks can be converted into one M4B file with chapter markers. The imported originals are retained. A sleep timer can stop playback after an interval or at the end of a chapter. Optional iCloud sync uses your own Apple account. Goodreads is also optional. A CSV import demonstrates separate reading shelves without signing in. This concludes the original sample recording.
